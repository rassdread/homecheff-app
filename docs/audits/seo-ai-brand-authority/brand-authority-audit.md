# Brand Authority Audit

## Goal
Recognition of HomeCheff as its own brand entity for queries such as HomeCheff, HomeCheff.eu, Who/What is HomeCheff, marketplace, buurtmarkt, community, craftsmanship.

## What was strengthened
- Canonical entity description retained from Phase 2.3
- Expanded AI authority Q&A (who/what/why/difference/barter/delivery/second-hand)
- Brand search monitoring methodology (no fabricated volumes)
- Search Console / Bing checklists for entity + structured-data + brand-query reporting
- Off-page readiness for LinkedIn / Instagram / Facebook / press / citations / KG (no fake profiles)

## What was not done (correctly)
- No Knowledge Panel ownership claim
- No social sameAs URLs invented
- No reviews, ratings, user counts or partnerships invented
